Delete your TARAhut Haazri account

TARAhut Haazri, by TARAhut AI Builds (Sukhija Technoaid)

In the app

  1. Open TARAhut Haazri and sign in.
  2. Tap You (bottom right).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Your account is switched off and you are signed out on every phone straight away.

Without the app

Email support@tarahutaibuilds.com with the subject "Delete my Haazri account", your name, the phone number you sign in with and your company's name. Send it from the email address saved on your account so we can confirm it is you. We switch the account off within 2 working days and confirm by email. You can also ask your company's admin to switch your account off.

What is deleted, and when

DataWhat happens
Name, phone number, email, department, designationErased 30 days after you delete your account
Check-in and check-out selfiesErased (photo files deleted) after 30 days
Check-in and check-out locations and addressesErased after 30 days
Leave request reasonsErased after 30 days
Notification tokens and sign-in codesDeleted straight away (tokens) or after 30 days (codes)
Dates and times you worked, hours, leave dates, salary settingsKept by your employer for payroll and legal records, no longer linked to your name

The 30 days give your employer time to settle your final salary. Until then your account cannot be used or switched back on.
