Privacy Policy

TARAhut Haazri · Last updated 10 October 2026

TARAhut Haazri is a staff attendance app made by TARAhut AI Builds ("we", "us"). Your employer (the organisation that added you to the app, our client) uses it to record when and where you start and finish work. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Name, phone number, email address, department, designationWhen your admin adds youTo create your account and send you sign-in codes by email
Salary type and amountIf your admin enters themFor your employer's records. Only admins can see them
Selfie photoEach time you check in or check outSo your employer can confirm it was you who marked attendance
Precise location (latitude, longitude) and the street address it resolves toOnly at the moment you check in or check outSo your employer can confirm where attendance was marked
Check-in and check-out times, hours workedEach check-in and check-outTo build your attendance record
Call history (number, contact name, call type, duration, time)Android only, and only when you tap "Sync" on the Calls screen after granting permissionTo share your work calls with your employer. The iPhone app never reads call history

The app does not track your location in the background, does not read your photo library, does not show ads, and does not use any analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own attendance records in the app.
  • Admins in your organisation can see the staff list, attendance records, selfies and check-in locations, and can approve or reject attendance.
  • Service providers that run the app for us, only to provide the service: Supabase (database and photo storage), Vercel (servers) and Resend (sign-in code emails).
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

How it is protected

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Selfies are kept in private storage and are only shown through links that expire after one hour. Sign-in codes are stored hashed, expire after 5 minutes, and are blocked after repeated wrong attempts.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and attendance records while your account is active, so your employer has a complete attendance history. When your admin removes your account, or you ask us to, we delete your selfies, locations and call history within 30 days. Your employer may keep its own copies of attendance totals where the law requires it (for example for payroll).

Your choices and rights

  • You can refuse camera or location permission, but you will not be able to check in or out without them.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting your admin or emailing us at the address below. We reply within 30 days. This includes your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • The app is for staff of organisations that use it and is not meant for children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
