TARAhut Haazri support

Need help? Email support@tarahutaibuilds.com and we will reply within two working days.

I can't sign in

Your admin has to add you first, with your phone number and email. Enter that phone number in the app and a 4-digit code is emailed to you. Check your spam folder; the code expires after 5 minutes.

Check-in says camera or location is required

Open the iPhone Settings app, find Haazri, and allow Camera and Location ("While Using the App").

I checked in by mistake

Ask your admin. They can reject the attendance record for that day.
