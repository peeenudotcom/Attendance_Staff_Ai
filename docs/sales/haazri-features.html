Your rules

Set up the way your business runs

Shifts
Morning, evening or night shifts, each with its own start, end and late time. Night shifts that cross midnight work too.
Late & half day
Late after the time you choose. Fewer hours than your limit counts as a half day; leaving early is flagged.
Weekly off
Any day of the week, paid.
Holidays
Your own list (Diwali, Dussehra, Gurpurab…), shown on every calendar and paid.
Location lock
Check-in only within a set distance of the office, for example 200 m. Field staff can be exempted.
Leave
Paid or unpaid; weekly offs and holidays aren't counted as leave days.
Salary from attendance

Pay worked out every month

Pay typeHow it's calculated
MonthlySalary ÷ days in month × paid days (worked + weekly offs + holidays + paid leave; half days count ½)
WeeklyWeekly pay ÷ 7 for each paid day
DailyDaily rate × days worked
HourlyHourly rate × hours worked

Optional late penalty: every few lates (you choose how many) cut half a day.

Advances and adjustments: record an advance already paid, a bonus or a deduction. The sheet shows earned from attendance, adjustments and the balance payable for each person.

Month close: before closing, Haazri lists missing check-outs and leave or corrections still waiting. Once closed, the salary sheet is saved and the month is locked, so later pay or shift changes don't alter it. Reopening needs a reason and is recorded with who did it.
