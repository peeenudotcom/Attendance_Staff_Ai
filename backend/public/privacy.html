Privacy Policy

TARAhut Haazri · Last updated 21 October 2026

TARAhut Haazri is a staff attendance app made by TARAhut AI Builds, a brand of SUKHIJA TECHNO AID ("we", "us"). Your employer (the organisation that added you to the app, our client) uses it to record when and where you start and finish work. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy

Salary type and amountIf your admin enters themFor your employer's records. Only admins can see them

The app does not track your location in the background, does not read your photo library, contacts or call history, does not show ads, has no in-app purchases or payments, and does not use any analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own attendance records in the app.
  • Admins in your organisation can see the staff list, attendance records, selfies and check-in locations, and can approve or reject attendance.
  • We (TARAhut AI Builds) look at a company's data only to run the service or when its admin asks us for help.
  • Service providers that run the app for us, only to provide the service: Supabase (database and photo storage), Vercel (servers), Resend (emails), Meta (sign-in codes sent on WhatsApp to your phone number), Amazon Web Services in Mumbai (comparing check-in and check-out selfies when your employer uses face match; the photos are not kept) and Expo, Apple and Google (delivering notifications).
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

How it is protected

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Selfies are kept in private storage and are only shown through links that expire after one hour. Sign-in codes are stored hashed, expire after 5 minutes, and are blocked after repeated wrong attempts.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and attendance records while your account is active, so your employer has a complete attendance history, and closed months keep a saved copy of that month's salary sheet. If your admin switches your account off, your records are kept for your employer until you or your employer ask us to delete them.

When you delete your account, it is switched off at once and, 30 days later, we erase your name, phone number, email, selfies, check-in locations and leave and correction reasons. Your employer keeps the dates and hours you worked, without your name, for payroll and legal records. See how to delete your account.

Your choices and rights

  • You can refuse camera or location permission, but you will not be able to check in or out without them.
  • You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings, and check-in reminders off in the app (You → Check-in reminders).
  • You can delete your account in the app (You → Delete my account) or by email (details).
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting your admin or emailing us at the address below. We reply within 30 days. This includes your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • The app is for staff of organisations that use it and is not meant for children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
